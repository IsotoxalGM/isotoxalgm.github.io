# Day-Night Cycle

Tethys is tidally-locked to Saturn, giving it an odd 45 hour daylight cycle.

- Local 00:00 is sunrise, with 95% Saturnshine. PVEDs are disabled.
- Local 10:00 is solar noon, with Saturnshine waning to 50%
- Local 17:00 the setting Sun along with Tethys' position in Saturn's magnetosphere begins to ionize snow particles.  This begins the Haze period where surface visibility is reduced to a few meters.
- Local 18:50 is solar eclipse when the Sun falls behind Saturn.  This is total begins a near 6 hour period of total night.
- Local 20:00 is when Active Night Enhanced Vision Devices are illuminated.  These multi-spectrum lamps "illuminate" the green region of the visibility map and begin to de-ionize the haze causing it to slowly settle.
- Local 24:30 begins Saturnrise.
- Local 30:00 AVEDs has knocked the haze down to the surface and there is enough Saturnshine to switch to Passive Vision Enhancement Devices which provide much better(further) vision than AVEDs.
- Local 33:50 Saturnshine waxes to 50%

# Visibility Map
This map is viewable on roll20.

JIS has provided us with official Visibility Map used by Tethys Security.

- Green regions are illuminated by AVEDs during AVED hours. AVED Vision range is approx 100m.  AVEDs can spot active shields up to 1000 meters.
- Yellow regions indicate areas visible during Sunlit hours and via PVEDS. PVEDs can spot active shields anywhere in green (+0), yellow(-10) or orange(-30) regions.
- Orange regions indicate areas obscured by terrain or the constant-haze of scraper operations. PVEDs can see into this region at -30.
- Red regions valleys, chasms, ravines, and depressions that are always obscured from view of other surface units.  However, during Sunlit hours, orbital units can see into this region if the objects are large enough or moving fast enough.
- Pink regions indicate perpetual haze kicked up by scraper operations. objects at surface level (under 10m) are unobservable by anything other than orbital doppler.

Orbital doppler radar arrays track all objects (drone size or larger) moving more than 25 kph (Move 20, 7 m/s).

##### Track Safety Monitor
TSM is an always-on system that anticipates hazards to rail cars. We know its watches for ice buildup but we dont know if it tracks personnel nearby or far above and below the rail it watches, if it does at all.  

Crossing a rail will require an encounter with the TSM.  With any luck, you can cross under or above the rail at a distance it wont care about, or you are moving too slow for it to care, or you are too small for it to care, or there are no trains near enough for it to care.  It's a giant unknown.

# JIS Sled 
JIS has access to a surface mobility sled "acquired" from Monolith storage.  The sled can carry 2 people (though it has a cargo bin that can fit 2 more).

The sled has a Monolith transponder that coordinates with the Station Traffic Manager.  Sleds following registered flight plans are largely ignored by doppler radar hits.  

To use the sled without being noticed, you will need to insert a flight plan into the Traffic Manager without raising any alarms.

The sled is currently hidden in a ravine below Core Ops.
