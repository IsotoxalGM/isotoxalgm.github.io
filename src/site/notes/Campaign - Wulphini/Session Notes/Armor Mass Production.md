# Crystal Court
All armor in the Crystal Series is by definition Unregistered, making it illegal in most jurisdictions. Should an authority discover its active nanotech, elevate this to extremely illegal.

###### Crystal Prince

Crystal Prince armor is indistinguishable from heavy security armor without an active scan.  Additionally its coloration, markings, and insignia can be altered as a complex action with +20 to Deception Skill Check when someone suspects it might be a forgery.
