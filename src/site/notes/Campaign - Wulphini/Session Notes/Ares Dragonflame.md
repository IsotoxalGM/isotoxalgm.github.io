### Ares Dragonflame Xm424, Articulated Light Autocannon
This light cannon rides on a stabilized articulated armature fitted to any armor of light weight or higher (Typically anything with 200+ magnitude, though certain VIP armored clothing meets the magnitude, armored clothing cannot be used as a mount).  The armature connects to the threaded fitting just forward of the pistol grip and contains the ammo feed and power needed to operate the cannon.  The entire hip-mounted stability package can be fitted non-destructively to any applicable armor with basic tools in about 10 minutes.  The ammoblock and powercell are housed in the stability package, not the cannon itself.

##### Ammoblock Configurations
==Copper==
The standard ammoblock material is nanospore-copper, a special form of metallic elemental copper printed by a nanoforge.  This material is the standard ammo the weapon system was designed around.  
Ammo Capacity: 900

==Gold==
This particular cannon has an experimental capacity to utilize nanospore-gold, a special form of metallic elemental gold printed by a nanoforge.  Loading a gold ammoblock alters the weapon characteristics as follows:
Ammo Capacity: 900
Magnitude: +10
Accuracy: -10
Damage: +2
Thermal 4, Impact 6

Rapidfire 4, Steady(No Accuracy penalty due to movement)
In this mode, all three barrels are utilized, cycling through a rotary pattern. While attacking with this mode under the influence of a Grit Chain, the attacker can offer one of the following Distractions to one ally without additional use of Grit: Swap Position, Maneuver, Rally, or Taunt.  Additionally, the Double Tap special ability upgrades to Triple Tap with this fire mode, if all three targets are within 30 degrees of arc.
