# Spring Break

Arriving at Brandmauer after setting the score with Frank on Freeride, Rama greets you with a rather unexpected bit of news.  You have been officially ordered to take time away from active missions.  You are allowed to provide intel and passive support to the Comet Mission through you contacts, CI's, and tasks but there will be no active missions for you for many weeks.  Firewall is concerned you might have too large a presence too close to too many major events recently.  Other teams will be active in your place.

##### Downtime
Rama is clear in his order: you are not to participate in any active missions until June at the earliest.  You are free to engage in any personal or professional activities during this time, even if this takes you off-station, as long as you remain completely under the radar acting as a private citizen in a manner that befits your established lifestyle and profession.

**==This gives you 12 weeks of downtime.==**  

Rama is expecting you to report back to your mission planning offices on Brandmauer by June 2nd, 190 to begin training for the active phase of the Skamander Lekythos Expansion.

###### Downtime Options
- You can use your downtime normally, using standard task rules.
- You can "return to work" in 1 month blocks, using 4 weeks of downtime to roll extra Salary (Roll twice, use the outcome you like best, this is in addition to normal 1st of the month rolls)
- You can Study. Gain +10 in 4 Knowledge skills or +20 in 1 Knowledge skill. This uses 8 weeks of downtime
- You can Train.  Gain +5 in 4 Active skills, or +10 in in Active skill. This uses 8 weeks of downtime. 
- You can  Practice.  Gain a Learning Opportunity on any skill you choose, roll its success normally. This uses 1 week of downtime.
- You can work on a Side Project.  If there is a specific activity you want to engage in, we'll figure out how much time it takes and remove that time from your available downtime.

### Lekythos
Since your team has not yet made contact with the Combe Family as part of the Skamander effort, another team has.  Unfortunately, this contact did little more than fan the flames of the Combes' desire for a habitat of their own.  They redoubled their efforts in acquiring one from Eden, and in doing so, uncovered an embarrassing Eden secret: The Lekythos.

There are 34 Habitats in the Sol System.

Habitats are not built. They are grown.  A "seed" is created in one of the Jupiter habitats.  This seed is a 50 ton lump of biotech precursor cells that has no awareness or sentience yet.  The seed is placed into the orbit it will have as a fully grown habitat.  Over the next 20 years, the caretakers of the seed bring material to the it in the form of small rocky asteroids and icy cometary fragments.  The seed consumes this material and grows into the massive hollow cylindrical form the public knows them as.  It takes more than a decade for the seed to gain awareness of itself and begin to form a personality.  Once it does, the complex systems of its interior that allow for a stable society of millions begins to grow with its walls as it inflates its interior.

The secret Eden has kept from the rest of the system is not all Habitat seeds mature correctly.  Eden has actually germinated 36 seeds for Sol to date.  Two have failed to gain awareness and sentience, therefore they never grew intelligent enough to grow their internal life support systems and biome maintenance facilities.  In short, there are two malformed, half-inflated "dead" habitats in solar orbit.  Eden calls these failed habs the *Lekythos*. The Combes' have learned of this, and now they want to buy one, in exchange for their silence on the matter.

Our team has inserted themselves in between Eden and the Combes, acting as an astroengineering firm capable of designing and installing the artificial biome in place of the biotech systems Lekythos never grew.  The negotiations and red tape is keeping everyone busy for now. We don't expect to start any actual work until the July-August.

The team we have posing as the engineering firm are not actual engineers, and certainly has no engineering facilities to build the kind of systems that will be needed.  For now the goal of that team is to be people the Combes call when they want an update on how well their hab is coming. 

Firewall, and you, still need the means to convert it into a working station, get it into position, and do that without the Combes knowing what is happening with it or where it actually is.
