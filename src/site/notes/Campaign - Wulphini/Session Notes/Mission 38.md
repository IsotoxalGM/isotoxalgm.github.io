# Lekythos

The Lekythos is not a small object.  Even now, inert and devoid of atmosphere, it has a mass of roughly 60 billion tons.  We will also need to bring in a few million tons of ice to create an atmosphere for the biome.

We can't just grapple it with a few dozen ships and tow it slowly. The drive plumes of that many ships burning in the same spot is detectable and would raise questions. "Why are there 40 ships burning hard but going nowhere?"

What we need are a couple of massive low temp super-freighter engines.  They are much harder to spot.  Even if detected, it will look like the drive of a super-freighter burning slowly, as they usually do.  

# Freighters

![Pasted image 20260908230450.png](/img/user/_meta/_attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260908230450.png)

A private convoy of three *Portsmouth*-class super-freighters departed from a site in the Greek asteroid belt pushing 450 million tons of ore (150 million each).  They began their departure burn at Point 1, near the Greeks.  They will continue to burn until Point 2.  At Point 2, the three ships will be out of reaction mass. 

Between Point 2 and Point 3, the convoy drifts.  During this time, the freight company sends a small fleet of haulers (likely out of Pallas) to refill the freighters' store of reaction mass for the upcoming braking burn.

At Point 3, the convoy begins its braking burn, ending at Point 4 near the Trojans where their destination lies.

##### Snow Crash

What the crews don't know is that while loading ore in the Greeks, a Firewall team loaded a malware package into each of the three ship's core systems.  We call this package *Snow Crash*.

# Pirates!

Silent Eternity has just rescued the crew of a vessel that fell victim to piracy.  Silent Eternity also engaged and captured the crew of 2 of the 3 pirate vessels.  

The rescued crew and the pirate detainees have been transferred to a police cutter coming from Pallas.  This cutter is burning toward Pallas now (Green arrow toward Pallas).  Silent Eternity is drifting along the path of its last intercept with the third pirate vessel (White arrow).

## Begin
This is where the next session begins.

# More Pirates!

It's at this point in time when the convoy of three super-freighters issue a distress call (from Red dot), indicating an imminent pirate attack.  The distress call states the three vessels are disconnecting from the ore carrier and splitting off into 3 separate vectors.  From this point on we will call the three freighters C1, C2, and C3.

C1, C2, and C3 burn hard to radically alter course, now that they are free of the massive ore carrier.  After a severe course correction, all three cut engines and run as dark as they can.

> [!tip] Snow Crash
> Snow Crash will alert the crew of these escape vectors as part of a recommended course of action in case of emergency. Firewall doesn't think the crews will be competent enough (or paid enough) to countermand this plan.  We are confident the crews will respond as their systems tell them to.

C2 reports a fast flyby of a small vessel, braking hard for an intercept with C1.  C2 reports being hit by kinetic shells from extreme range, taking only superficial damage.

> [!tip] Snow Crash
> C2 doesn't actually see anything and was not hit by anything. Snow Crash injected false impact reports and a phantom contact to ship sensors.

C1 will report they are being boarded.

C3 Remains dark.
> [!tip] Snow Crash
> C3 is running dark because Snow Crash has crashed their entire system core and taken engines and reactors offline.

Silent Eternity responds to the distress call with a high burn intercept vector.  The police cutter in the region is carrying the rescued crew and detained pirates, and is therefore unable to burn as hard as Silent Eternity. 

Since C3 is dark, Silent Eternity can report the assumption that dark means hiding and if they were in trouble they would have called for help.

Since C2 is reporting only superficial hull damage, Silent Eternity can respond directly to the pirate action taking place at C1.

### Firewall's Plan

##### C1 
Silent Eternity arrives at C1 after the pirates have left.  The boarding party crashed the system core, leaving C1 disabled and adrift.  The boarders also badly injured several of C1's crew.  

Silent Eternity is to arrive with medical assistance and to help restore the ship's core network.  Once back online, C1 is able to run for a friendly port under its own power, or chase down the ore carrier if they wish.

C2 will report a sighting of the pirate vessel.

> [!tip] Snow Crash
> The sighting report is a false signature injected by Snow Crash.

##### C2
Silent Eternity is to burn hard toward C2 and the reported Pirate Contact.  On arrival, Silent Eternity will report to the crew of C2 that C2's reactor is giving off dangerously erratic gamma radiation, indicative of containment failure.  Silent Eternity must convince the crew of C2 that their systems are compromised and they need to abandon ship immediately.

> [!tip] Snow Crash
> At a pre-determine time that will be made know to Silent Eternity. Snow Crash will jettison a small container that was hidden on the hull by the Snow Crash team at the Greeks.  This container will drift a few hundred kilometers away and then detonate a few milligrams of antimatter.

With the crew of C2 in life boats, or aboard Silent Eternity, the crew of Silent Eternity is to convince them the explosion was the result of critical containment failure of C2's damaged reactor and that the entire ship was vaporized.  The limited sensor package on a life boat, or the limited access to Silent Eternity's internal system should make this an easy lie to sell.

Silent Eternity will then "spot" the pirate contact, on a similar vector taken by C3.  

If the crew of C2 are in life boats, leave them there with the excuse that a police cutter is only a few hours away.  If Silent Eternity has taken the crew of C2 on board, put them into medical stasis in order to survive the hard maneuvering of the upcoming fight.

Silent Eternity will burn to intercept.  Silent Eternity is to create a false log of a long chase followed by brief combat with a pirate vessel, ending with the total destruction of the pirate due to a railgun round causing reactor containment failure.  The crew of C2 will not be witness to this deception because they will be far away, floating in nearly blind life boats, or in medical comas "for their protection".  C1 will be too far away to see anything with their patched up ship systems.

> [!tip] Snow Crash
> Snow Crash autopilot will slowly pilot C2 to a rendezvous point somewhere in the outer reaches of the Belt.

##### C3
Silent Eternity will use the time "Chasing the pirate" to actually burn toward C3 (coordinates given by Snow Crash via tight beam once it detects the explosion near C2).

Silent Eternity is to board C3 ***as pirates***.  Disable all crew members. Disabable all comms, beacons, tracking systems, and navigation on a life boat.  Put the incapacitated crew into this life boat and launch it. A vessel carrying the Snow Crash team will intercept the life boat and gather the incapacitated crew.

Snow Crash will autopilot C3 to a rendezvous point somewhere in the outer reaches of the Belt.

Silent Eternity is to return to the life boats of C2, or to C1 if the crew of C2 was taken aboard Silent Eternity.  Silent Eternity is then to await the arrival of the police cutter to offer a full report, including the falsified logs of the fake chase and battle, and the complete lack of any detection of where C3 might have gone.  Possibly hint that other pirates may have been out there, and got to C3 without being spotted.

Firewall gains two super-freighter hulls we can use to move the Lekythos and the ice needed to feed its Biome.
