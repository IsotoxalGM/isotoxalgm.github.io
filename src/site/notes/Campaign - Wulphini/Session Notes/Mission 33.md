# O Windless, Wingless Sky 

It has been months now since the first of the Barbary Refugees were discovered. Months since the capture of the rogue Desolation.  You've traced the edges of "The Project" from its offices of Messis Copiosa.  You've destroyed the command station, captured its senior commanders, recovered the malignant biotech.  You've given Brandmauer much to work with in unraveling this mystery.

Brandmauer has been busy!

#### The Frost Line
There are several thousand asteroids locked in Jupiter's L4 that are larger 50km in diameter. Like the Main Belt asteroids, there is usually hundreds of thousands of kilometers between these bodies.  Unlike the Main Belt rocks, these orbit beyond Sol's frost line: the distance at which even direct sunlight is not warm enough to melt or boil away volatile compounds.  Main belt rocks have had billions of years to boil off dangerous gasses and unstable ice.  This is why the vast majority of Belters live in the Main belt.

The rocks found in the Greeks and Trojans are full of dangerous ice compounds just barely cold enough to remain frozen: carbon monoxide, hydrogen cyanide, hydrogen sulfide, elemental chlorine and fluorine, and a long list of others. Settlement on these rocks requires extremely careful management of waste heat and rigorous maintenance schedules with very low tolerance for error.  It's simply not economical yet.  Maybe in a century or two, when Humanity needs more real estate, these rocks will see more traffic.

Until then, however, the Greeks and Trojans are home to very few people.  This makes them perfect for industry.  Lots of raw metals, water ice, carbon, oxygen, and very few local residents, means several nations use these rocks for deep space factories... or even secret shipyards.

#### Secret Shipyard
Thanks to all the data you've supplied Brandmauer, we've discovered Venus has a shipyard in the Greeks whose work output doesn't quite match up with published reports.  What they claim to be a simple repair/refit/resupply shipyard seems to have nearly completed a new capital ship.  *O Windless, Wingless Sky* is a heavy battlecruiser, the first of this particular design.

What's more, it doesn't seem like *OWWS* has Monolith or Titan engines.  Even GovCentral buys Monolith or Titan engines.  Either Venus has its own engine technology (highly unlikely, given the capabilities of their culture), or they stole someone's engines (highly likely, given the capabilities of their culture).  Neither Titan nor Monolith will be very happy about that.

Furthering the insult, Venus seems to believe *OWWS* has a stealth-capable hull, something that is universally banned via international treaty.

Luckily (and unsurprisingly), Venus has not been able to secure the biotech brain needed to operate such a vessel.  Venus seems to have forgotten how difficult it is to lie/cheat/negotiate in bad faith when you are dealing with a sales rep backed by a million minds.  It looks like Venus is actually planning to steal one.  Eden in not happy.  It seems you've accidentally uncovered the source of growing tensions between Venus and Eden in the last year, and perhaps why Eden had an actual spy infiltrate one of their cloud cities.

#### Lights on On, but nobody's Home
It seems as if there is a fully operational, state of the art, heavy battlecruiser ready to sail.... if they only had a brain installed to make it all work.  They can't even crew up the vessel and start shake-downs and training ops.  

There is a crew present, though currently it is technical and managerial in nature. Tech reps, engineers, inspectors, senior flag officers looking to sign their name on the documents and name things for themselves.  The serving line officers and enlisted ratings are starting to arrive now, though not in great numbers just yet.

Still, its not like we can send in 6 people to overpower 500 people to steal a ship with no brain.  

We have an idea though.  We have the beginnings of a plan.  Better still, we have an angry Eden supplying us with a brain.  What we need is a team of 6 that includes someone with affinity, and the technical skills handling biotech to install this brain, wake it, and convince it that its a Firewall agent now.  That team needs to include a system operator skilled enough to keep the crew locked out of essential systems during the heist.  That team needs to be able to defend the bridge from security forces attempting to restore control.  That team also needs a pilot crazy enough to want to solo fly a 235,000 ton untested hull designed to be flown by a bridge crew of 12 and ship compliment of 2400.  Surprise surprise, your team qualifies.

Of course there's still the matter of several hundred people there determined to make sure you can't do any of that, but we're working on it.
